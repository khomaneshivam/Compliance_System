# ADR 0003: AI Proposals vs. Human Sign-Off Boundaries

## Status
**Accepted**

## Context
PlantProof uses OCR and Generative AI (LLMs / Vision models) to accelerate document verification and regulatory change comprehension:
- Optical Character Recognition extracts certificate numbers, validity dates, equipment tags, and issuing authorities from scanned documents.
- LLMs summarize dense government gazette notifications into plain English and propose candidate enterprise impacts.

In regulatory compliance, false positives or false negatives carry severe consequences (statutory non-compliance, equipment seizure, operational shutdowns). Completely autonomous AI decision-making is legally unacceptable.

## Decision
We enforce the foundational architectural principle: **"AI Proposes, Humans Dispose"**.

1. **State Machine Isolation:**
   - Any data generated by AI/OCR is initialized with status `PROPOSED` or `PENDING_REVIEW`.
   - AI outputs are never committed directly to the active Requirements Register.
2. **Confidence Ratings:**
   - Every extracted field carries an explicit confidence score ($0.000$ to $1.000$) rendered as a visible indicator (**High** $\ge 0.85$, **Medium** $0.60-0.84$, **Low** $< 0.60$).
3. **Mandatory Human Approval:**
   - Only an authenticated user possessing appropriate RBAC permissions (`EHS_MANAGER`, `PLANT_MANAGER`, or `ADMIN`) can transition an evidence document to `APPROVED`.
   - The user must inspect the document preview side-by-side with the extracted fields in the Dual-Pane Review UI and has the ability to edit any misread parameters before approval.
4. **Audit Immutability:**
   - Every human approval, modification, or rejection writes an immutable entry into `audit_logs` capturing user identity, previous vs. new values, rationale, and UTC timestamp.

## Consequences
### Positive
- Strict legal defensibility: enterprise compliance cannot be compromised by generative model hallucinations or OCR misreadings.
- Builds trust with factory managers and external statutory auditors.
- Eliminates liability from automated legal interpretation.

### Negative / Trade-offs
- Requires human labor to review documents and changes. (Mitigated by pre-filling high-confidence fields and providing ergonomic side-by-side review workflows).
